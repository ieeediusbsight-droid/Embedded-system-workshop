What You'll Learn

Step-by-step skills to build your own Home Automation system

⚙️

Arduino & ESP32 Basics

GPIO control, digital I/O, PWM, and microcontroller programming — the foundation of your Home Automation project.

🌡️

Sensors & Smart Devices

Temperature, humidity, motion & relay modules — control real home appliances like lights and fans automatically.

📱

WiFi & App Control

Connect your home system to WiFi, control devices via smartphone app and MQTT — real wireless automation in action.

🏠

Home Automation Project

Build a fully working Home Automation system — control lights, fans & sensors remotely from your phone!
